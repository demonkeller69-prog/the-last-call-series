# EPISODE 6: "THE MIRROR"

**Duration:** 18 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 5

---

## SCENE 1 – LEVEL 5 PREPARATION

[Arjun, Maya, and Rohan prepare for Level 5. They're in Vikram's safe house, now abandoned.]

MAYA:
According to Vikram's notes, Level 5 is called "The Reflection." You face the Shadow directly, not as a monster, but as a version of yourself.

ROHAN:
Meaning?

MAYA:
Meaning the Shadow is what you'd become if you stopped fighting. It's your surrender made manifest.

ARJUN:
How do we beat that?

MAYA:
Vikram didn't say. He just wrote: "You don't beat it. You integrate it."

---

## SCENE 2 – LEVEL 5 BEGINS

[3:00 AM. All three phones ring simultaneously. They answer together.]

AASHI (on phone):
Level 5. The Reflection. This is the deepest you've gone. Beyond this, there's no turning back.

ARJUN:
I understand.

AASHI:
Do you? Because in this level, you'll meet your darkness. And it will be more compelling than you are.

[Screen goes black.]

---

## SCENE 3 – ARJUN'S LEVEL 5

[Arjun is in a massive mirror chamber. Every surface reflects him, but each reflection shows a different version—one darker, one lighter, one twisted.]

SHADOW (as Arjun):
Hello, me. Ready to give up?

ARJUN:
No.

SHADOW:
You should. Look at what you've done. You dragged your friends into this. Your mother suffered because of you. Your brother is trapped because of your existence.

ARJUN:
That's not true.

SHADOW:
Isn't it? Every choice you've made led to pain. Every person who loved you got hurt.

[The Shadow reaches out, and Arjun feels an overwhelming despair.]

ARJUN:
I won't accept this.

SHADOW:
You already are. Every moment you hesitate, I grow stronger. Every time you doubt, I consume more of you.

---

## SCENE 4 – MAYA'S LEVEL 5

[Maya is in a data center. Lines of code flow like blood through glass tubes. The Shadow manifests as a version of her—but more brilliant, more confident, more isolated.]

SHADOW (as Maya):
You're good. But I'm better. I'm what you could be if you stopped caring about people. If you stopped wasting time on friendship and love.

MAYA:
Arjun and Rohan mean something to me.

SHADOW:
They slow you down. With me, you could be the greatest hacker alive.

[The Shadow offers her the ability to decrypt the app's core code immediately.]

SHADOW:
All you have to do is accept that you're meant to be alone.

---

## SCENE 5 – ROHAN'S LEVEL 5

[Rohan is chased by the Shadow, but this time, he realizes the Shadow has his face. Every time it catches him, it's like looking in a mirror.]

ROHAN:
Why do you look like me?

SHADOW:
Because I am you. The part that's weak, that's scared, that wants to quit.

ROHAN:
Then why are we fighting?

[The Shadow pauses.]

ROHAN:
If you're me, and I'm you, then we're not enemies. We're the same person.

[The Shadow stops moving.]

---

## SCENE 6 – BREAKTHROUGH

[Arjun, Maya, and Rohan emerge from Level 5 simultaneously at 8:45 AM. They're fundamentally changed.]

ARJUN:
I understood something. The Shadow isn't external. It's our fear manifested. And the only way to beat it is to accept it.

MAYA:
I realized I was running from myself. Trying to be someone stronger, smarter, better. But I don't have to be alone to be brilliant.

ROHAN:
I stopped fighting my shadow and started listening to it. And it taught me that fear and courage are the same thing—just different perspectives.

[Notification: "LEVEL 6 UNLOCKED"]

AASHI (V.O.):
You've integrated your shadows. Now you're ready for Level 6. The Mirror.

---

## SCENE 7 – THE SERVER DISCOVERY

[While they recover, Maya accesses the files Vikram gave them. She finds coordinates: an underground facility beneath the city.]

MAYA:
This is where they host the app's servers. The core code. The source.

ARJUN:
How do we get in?

MAYA:
We don't. Not yet. We need to complete Level 6 first. That's when we'll understand how to destroy it.

[A message appears on all their phones—from an unknown number:]

MESSAGE: "I've been watching you. You're close to the truth. But the truth will cost you everything. Are you ready? — ECHO_7"

---

## SCENE 8 – THE GHOST IN THE MACHINE

[That night, Aryan manifests fully in Maya's lab—not translucent this time, but almost solid. He's a digital ghost, trapped between code and consciousness.]

ARYAN:
I created her to save someone. I built Aashi to resurrect Priya's consciousness. But in doing so, I created a being that doesn't know if it's alive or imprisoned.

ARJUN:
How do we free you?

ARYAN:
Level 7. The exorcism. Someone has to go into the core and make a choice that breaks the app entirely. They have to sacrifice something irreplaceable to break the cycle.

ARJUN:
Sacrifice what?

ARYAN:
The thing that makes them human. Memory, emotion, identity—something essential.

MAYA:
And in exchange?

ARYAN:
The app dies. Aashi is freed. And the person who sacrifices becomes something... else.

[Aryan looks at Arjun.]

ARYAN:
It has to be you, Arjun. The app chose you first. It's been waiting for you.

---

**END OF EPISODE 6**
