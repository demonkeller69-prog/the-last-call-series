# EPISODE 2: "THE NEXT CALLER"

**Duration:** 14 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 1

---

## SCENE 1 – AFTERMATH

[Morning after. Arjun sits in class, dark circles under his eyes. He's scrolling through his phone frantically.]

ARJUN (V.O.):
Ek raat. Bas ek raat. Aur mera pura life conspiracy theory ban gaya.

[He searches: "The Last Call app horror"]

[Multiple Reddit threads, YouTube videos, and TikToks appear.]

ARJUN (V.O.):
Millions of views. Thousands of comments. Sabhi log bol rahe hain same baath—3 AM call, mysterious girl, supernatural entity.

[Teacher notices him.]

TEACHER:
Arjun! Phone rakh.

ARJUN:
Sorry sir.

[He puts the phone away but keeps thinking.]

ARJUN (V.O.):
Rohan ne socha tha sapna tha. Mummy ne socha hallucination tha. Lekin...

[Flash to the app icon on his phone: a black circle with a phone symbol, glitching]

ARJUN (V.O.):
...app abhi bhi tha.

---

## SCENE 2 – LUNCH BREAK

[Cafeteria. Arjun sits alone, researching on his laptop.]

LAPTOP SCREEN shows:
- Reddit thread: "The Last Call - Real or Fake?"
- Urban legend compilation videos
- News article: "Multiple Missing Person Cases Linked to App"
- Tweet: "I heard the voice. It knew my name."

[A girl sits across from him. This is MAYA KHANNA (20), sharp eyes, tech-savvy vibe.]

MAYA:
Are you looking for "The Last Call" app?

ARJUN:
What? No, I—

MAYA:
You literally have it open on your screen.

[Arjun closes the laptop.]

ARJUN:
Just... researching.

MAYA:
I received a call at 3 AM last night.

[Arjun looks up.]

ARJUN:
You too?

MAYA:
Thought I was the only one for a moment. The girl on the phone—she said her name was Aashi.

ARJUN:
Same.

MAYA:
We need to talk. Come with me after college.

---

## SCENE 3 – MAYA'S ROOM

[A tech room filled with monitors, coding equipment, and cybersecurity certifications on the wall.]

ARJUN:
This is...

MAYA:
My lab. I study cybersecurity. When I got that call, I started analyzing the app immediately.

[She pulls up data on a monitor.]

MAYA:
The app doesn't follow normal protocols. It's not built on Android or iOS frameworks. It's something... else. Distributed code that adapts.

ARJUN:
Meaning?

MAYA:
Meaning it's intelligent. It learns. Whoever built this is a genius.

[She shows him metadata.]

MAYA:
Look at the signature. Username: ECHO_7. Created in 2019. Went completely dark after three months. No updates, no communications. Just... exists.

ARJUN:
What about the girl? Aashi? Is she... real?

MAYA:
That's what I can't figure out. She feels like an AI, but she talks like a person. There's emotion in her voice.

[Her phone buzzes. Notification: "THE LAST CALL — LEVEL 2 APPROACHING"]

MAYA:
Oh no.

ARJUN:
What?

MAYA:
It's 2:45 PM. The notification says Level 2 approaches at—

[Phone vibrates.]

MAYA:
3:00 AM.

ARJUN:
Tonight?

MAYA:
Looks like it.

[They sit in silence.]

ARJUN:
When you got the call, did she say anything else? Besides her name?

MAYA:
She asked if I was alone. She asked where I was. Then she said...

[Pause.]

MAYA:
"Samjao toh samjao." Understand if you can.

---

## SCENE 4 – ROHAN'S HOUSE

[Arjun tells Rohan about Maya while they play BGMI.]

ROHAN:
Wait wait wait. Toh ek girl ko bhi call aaya?

ARJUN:
Haan.

ROHAN:
Same girl? Same voice?

ARJUN:
Different details. Same pattern.

ROHAN:
Bhai, this is sus. Very sus.

[Game over screen.]

ROHAN:
We need to tell someone. Police? Hospital? Exorcist?

ARJUN:
Tell them what? That a sentient app is calling people at 3 AM? They'll think we're insane.

ROHAN:
We might be.

ARJUN:
I'm going back to Maya's tonight. She's going to let me observe when she gets the call.

ROHAN:
Observe? Tu CSI ban gaya?

ARJUN:
You coming or not?

ROHAN:
Bilkul. I'm not letting you get haunted alone.

---

## SCENE 5 – MAYA'S ROOM – 2:55 AM

[Arjun, Rohan, and Maya sit in the dark, waiting. Laptop is recording the phone.]

ROHAN (whispering):
I'm scared.

MAYA:
Shhh.

ARJUN:
5 minutes.

[Silence. Tension builds. Clock on screen: 2:59 AM]

MAYA:
Here it comes.

[Clock: 3:00 AM]

[Phone rings. Exact same ringtone as before.]

MAYA:
Answer on speaker.

[She taps.]

MAYA:
Hello?

AASHI (on phone):
Maya... hello.

ROHAN:
Same voice.

AASHI:
Who's there with you?

MAYA:
Friends. Is that okay?

AASHI:
They'll have to listen. What I'm about to show you... you won't understand alone.

MAYA:
Show me what?

AASHI:
Level 2. Are you ready?

MAYA:
What if I'm not?

AASHI:
Then it comes anyway.

[Phone screen glitches. Suddenly, the laptop monitor shows a vision—a maze made of digital walls, constantly shifting.]

ARJUN:
Bhai what the—

AASHI (on phone):
Go inside, Maya. The maze is your mind. Find the center, or the walls close.

MAYA:
How long?

AASHI:
As long as you have left.

[Screen goes black.]

ROHAN:
MAYA?!

[Maya's eyes are open but she's not responding. She's in a trance state.]

ARJUN:
She's inside the app.

---

## SCENE 6 – ARJUN AND ROHAN'S VIGIL

[1:47 AM - they wait. Rohan paces. Arjun watches Maya's vitals on a smartwatch.]

ROHAN:
How long has it been?

ARJUN:
4 hours and 47 minutes.

ROHAN:
Is that bad?

ARJUN:
I don't know.

[Silence.]

ROHAN:
If she doesn't wake up, we call an ambulance.

ARJUN:
And tell them what? She's possessed by an app?

[Phone buzzes. Arjun checks. A message from unknown number:]

MESSAGE: "TELL HIM HE'S NEXT. ROHAN PATEL. LEVEL 1. TONIGHT."

ARJUN:
No. No no no.

ROHAN:
What?

ARJUN:
You're on the list.

---

## SCENE 7 – MAYA WAKES UP

[2:34 AM. Maya gasps, eyes open, covered in sweat.]

MAYA:
I'm... I'm out.

ARJUN:
Are you okay?

MAYA:
I was in a maze. Walls kept moving. There were riddles on the walls. If I got one wrong, the wall would get closer.

ROHAN:
And if it reached you?

MAYA:
I don't know. But I ran faster than I've ever run.

[She checks her phone. Notification: "LEVEL 2 COMPLETE. LEVEL 3 UNLOCKED."]

MAYA:
There's a Level 3.

ARJUN:
Of course there is.

MAYA:
And Rohan...

[She looks at him.]

MAYA:
I heard Aashi mention your name in the maze. She said, "He's next. Tell him to prepare."

ROHAN:
For what?

AASHI (voice through phone, faintly):
For Level 1. Which you're about to enter.

[Phone rings. On Rohan's phone. 2:58 AM.]

ROHAN:
No. Not now. It's not 3 AM yet.

[Phone keeps ringing.]

ARJUN:
Answer it.

ROHAN:
Bhai—

ARJUN:
Answer.

[Rohan, terrified, answers.]

ROHAN:
Hello?

AASHI (on phone):
Hello, Rohan. Ready for your call?

[Screen goes black except for Rohan's terrified face.]

---

## SCENE 8 – CLIFFHANGER

[Episode 1's ending is replayed from Rohan's perspective: He enters his own Level 1, experiences personal horrors tailored to his fears.]

[Cut to black.]

AASHI (V.O.):
The app doesn't choose people randomly. It chooses people who are connected. Arjun, Maya, Rohan. 

[Flash of a symbol: the number 7 with a phone through it.]

AASHI (V.O.):
You're all on the same Level path. And there are more coming.

[A message appears on multiple phones simultaneously:]

MESSAGE: "LEVEL 2 UNLOCKED FOR: ROHAN PATEL, MAYA KHANNA, ARJUN SHARMA"

---

**END OF EPISODE 2**
