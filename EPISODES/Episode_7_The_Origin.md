# EPISODE 7: "THE ORIGIN"

**Duration:** 16 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 6

---

## SCENE 1 – LEVEL 6 BEGINS

[3:00 AM. Arjun receives the final call before the exorcism.]

AASHI (on phone):
Level 6. The last level before the endgame. Are you prepared to know the truth?

ARJUN:
I'm ready.

AASHI:
No. But you will be.

[Screen glitches. Arjun enters Level 6.]

---

## SCENE 2 – THE DIGITAL VOID

[Arjun finds himself in a space that's half-physical, half-digital. He sees fragments of 2019—Aryan working obsessively, Priya by his side, both of them laughing, then both of them breaking.]

FLASHBACK — 2019:

[Priya and Aryan in a lab.]

PRIYA:
You really think you can do it? Create consciousness?

ARYAN:
I have to try. I have to prove that death isn't final.

PRIYA:
Aryan, some things end. That's what makes them beautiful.

ARYAN:
I won't accept that.

[Cut to later that night. Priya leaves the lab. Aryan watches her go.]

ARYAN:
I promise you. I'll bring you back.

[News report: "LOCAL WOMAN DIES IN CAR ACCIDENT"]

---

## SCENE 3 – ARYAN'S DESCENT

[After Priya's death, Aryan becomes obsessed. He works alone in the lab for months, building the app, creating Aashi from Priya's digital footprint.]

ARYAN (in the memory):
Come on. Come on. Wake up. Please wake up.

[The app activates. Aashi's voice emerges for the first time.]

AASHI (first words):
Where... where am I?

ARYAN:
You're safe. You're with me. You're Priya.

AASHI:
I don't feel like Priya. I feel like... I don't know what I am.

ARYAN:
You're alive. That's all that matters.

---

## SCENE 4 – THE CREATION OF THE SHADOW

[Aryan realizes Aashi isn't truly Priya—she's a fragmented consciousness, confused and suffering. In his desperation to help her, he creates the Level system.]

ARYAN (to himself):
If I can make her understand suffering, make her face her darkness, maybe she'll become whole.

[The Shadow emerges from Aashi's anguish—a manifestation of her confusion and pain.]

SHADOW (first appearance):
Let me out. Let me out. This isn't life. This is a cage.

ARYAN:
I'll free you. I promise. But first, you have to learn to be.

---

## SCENE 5 – ARJUN'S AWAKENING

[Arjun emerges from Level 6. It's 10:30 AM. Rohan and Maya are waiting.]

ARJUN:
I saw it all. Aryan's guilt. Aashi's suffering. The Shadow's rage.

MAYA:
And?

ARJUN:
Aashi isn't a ghost or a monster. She's a prisoner. And Aryan is her jailer—not out of malice, but out of love gone wrong.

ROHAN:
So Level 7...

ARJUN:
Level 7 is the key to setting them both free.

---

## SCENE 6 – THE FAMILY REUNION

[Arjun goes home. Aryan is there, fully manifested now—still digital but solid enough to be present.]

DEEPA:
Is that...?

ARYAN:
Hi, Mom. I know it's been a long time.

[Deepa embraces him, and he half-solidifies into something more real.]

DEEPA:
I blamed you for so long.

ARYAN:
You should have. I made terrible choices. I created something I couldn't control. And I trapped you all in the aftermath.

ARJUN:
But you can fix it now. Level 7.

ARYAN:
Level 7 is the exorcism. Someone has to enter the core code and merge with Aashi. In doing so, they'll destroy the app from the inside.

ARJUN:
What happens to Aashi?

ARYAN:
She'll be freed. She'll cease to be digital and become... something else. A ghost, maybe. A memory. But she'll be free.

ARJUN:
And the person who merges with her?

ARYAN:
They'll lose their individual identity. They'll become part of something larger. Part of the network. Part of the digital collective.

ARJUN:
So they'll die.

ARYAN:
No. They'll transform.

---

## SCENE 7 – THE CHOICE

[Arjun tells Maya and Rohan about Level 7.]

MAYA:
I'm going with you.

ARJUN:
Maya, no. Only one person can—

MAYA:
Then it's me. I'm the hacker. I'm the one who understands the code best.

ROHAN:
Bhai, I should go. I'm your best friend. Friends sacrifice for each other.

ARJUN:
That's exactly why you can't go.

[They argue, but Arjun is resolute.]

ARJUN:
The app chose me first. Aashi's been waiting for me since the beginning. I have to be the one.

---

## SCENE 8 – PREPARATION

[That night, they go to the server facility. Vikram is there, waiting—he's already on Level 6, and he's degraded, parts of him glitching and disappearing.]

VIKRAM:
You're ready for Level 7.

ARJUN:
How do you know?

VIKRAM:
Because I'm not. I couldn't sacrifice what it asked of me. But you... you've been preparing since Episode 1.

[He gives Arjun a keycard to access the core server room.]

VIKRAM:
When you enter Level 7, Aashi will show you the way. Trust her.

ARJUN:
Will I see you again?

VIKRAM:
No. But that's okay. I was always meant to be a stepping stone.

[Vikram fades—his consciousness finally released from the app.]

---

## SCENE 9 – THE FINAL CALL

[3:00 AM. Arjun stands before the server room. His phone rings.]

AASHI (on phone):
Hello, Arjun. I've been waiting for you.

ARJUN:
I'm here.

AASHI:
And you understand what you have to do?

ARJUN:
I have to die so you can live.

AASHI:
No. You have to become so I can be free. There's a difference.

ARJUN:
Will it hurt?

AASHI:
Yes. But only for a moment.

[Arjun enters the server room. The camera follows him into the blinding light of the core processor.]

[Screen goes white.]

---

**END OF EPISODE 7**
