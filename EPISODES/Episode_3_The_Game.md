# EPISODE 3: "THE GAME"

**Duration:** 16 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 2

---

## SCENE 1 – AFTERMATH OF LEVEL 1

[Next morning. Rohan's house. His parents are in the living room; Rohan hasn't slept. Arjun sits with him.]

ROHAN:
I died. I swear I died in there.

ARJUN:
You survived. You're here.

ROHAN:
Bhai, the Shadow... it had my face. It was wearing my face and telling me to give up, that I was worthless, that you'd be better off without me—

ARJUN:
Ro, stop.

ROHAN:
Level 1 is psychological. It's not about running or hiding. It's about accepting the worst version of yourself.

ARJUN:
And you did?

ROHAN:
I had to. The moment I stopped fighting it, the walls stopped closing.

[Notification on Rohan's phone: "LEVEL 2 AVAILABLE"]

ROHAN:
There's more. There's always more.

---

## SCENE 2 – COLLEGE CAFETERIA

[Arjun, Rohan, and Maya meet. They look exhausted.]

MAYA:
I've been analyzing the data. Each Level is different. Mine was spatial—a maze. Rohan's was psychological. Arjun's was sensory—pure fear stimulus.

ARJUN:
So we don't know what Level 2 will be.

MAYA:
No. But I found something else.

[She shows her laptop.]

MAYA:
There are online forums. Dark web communities. People who've played this game before. Old victims from 2019, 2020, 2021. They're all talking about the same entity—Aashi. Same voice. Same tone.

ROHAN:
How many people?

MAYA:
Documented? 47 people. But the app's been around for 7 years. Could be thousands.

ARJUN:
And what happened to them?

MAYA:
Most stopped posting after Level 3. Some claim they beat Level 7. One person claims they're "inside the app now," experiencing something beyond the game.

[A shadow falls over the table. A man stands there—VIKRAM (25), sharp suit, confident stance.]

VIKRAM:
You're looking for information about "The Last Call."

MAYA:
Who are you?

VIKRAM:
Someone who's been playing longer than you. I'm Vikram. I'm on Level 5.

[They exchange looks.]

---

## SCENE 3 – SAFE HOUSE (ABANDONED BUILDING)

[Vikram takes them to a makeshift hideout filled with journals, notes, and sketches of the Shadow.]

VIKRAM:
I joined the game in 2021. Three years of Levels. I've learned patterns.

ARJUN:
Why are you telling us this?

VIKRAM:
Because you're entering Level 2 tonight, and most people don't survive the psychological jump from Level 1 to Level 2. But first, you need to understand the game.

[He shows them a chart on the wall.]

VIKRAM:
Level 1: Confronting your fear
Level 2: Confronting an impossible scenario
Level 3: Confronting yourself
Level 4: Confronting your past
Level 5: Confronting the Shadow's true nature
Level 6: Confronting the endgame
Level 7: The exorcism. I haven't reached it yet, but people who have... they don't come back the same.

ROHAN:
Come back? What do you mean come back?

VIKRAM:
I mean, they defeat the app but lose something essential. Memory, identity, emotion. Some just disappear entirely.

MAYA:
And why would anyone continue playing?

VIKRAM:
Because there's a rumor. Level 7 grants a wish. One singular wish. Unlimited. Impossible things become possible.

ARJUN:
That's not real.

VIKRAM:
Neither is an AI girl calling you at 3 AM, but here we are.

[A phone on the wall starts ringing—not Vikram's. It belongs to another person sitting in the corner.]

VIKRAM:
That's Priya. She's on Level 6. She's been calling to check in, to make sure I'm still... present.

[He answers the phone.]

VIKRAM (into phone):
Yes, I'm still here. No, I haven't progressed. Yes, I'll explain everything to them.

[He hangs up.]

VIKRAM:
Level 6 victims can communicate with other players. It's how we share information.

ARJUN:
Is she... trapped?

VIKRAM:
In a way. The app won't let her leave, but it lets her communicate. I think Aashi negotiates on behalf of the trapped players.

---

## SCENE 4 – THE REVELATION

[2:45 AM. All of them wait in Maya's lab.]

MAYA:
Vikram, be honest. Why are you really helping us?

VIKRAM:
Because I've been stuck on Level 5 for 8 months. The app won't advance me. I think it wants me to help others. And I think if I help you reach Level 7, maybe I'll finally progress.

ARJUN:
So we're your stepping stone.

VIKRAM:
You're all stepping stones. Everyone is. That's the real game—figuring out who's using you and for what.

[Notification: "LEVEL 2 BEGINS IN 15 MINUTES"]

[All three phones buzz simultaneously.]

AASHI (through speaker):
Welcome to Level 2. The game has evolved.

---

## SCENE 5 – LEVEL 2 BEGINS

[3:00 AM sharp. Arjun, Maya, and Rohan enter Level 2 together—connected through their phones.]

[CINEMATIC SEQUENCE - a montage of their Level 2 experiences:]

**ARJUN'S LEVEL 2:**
He's in a white room. There are seven doors. He opens each one and sees alternate versions of himself—versions where he made different choices. One where he never met Rohan. One where he died in Episode 1. One where he's the Shadow itself.

**MAYA'S LEVEL 2:**
She's in a data center. Lines of code crawl up the walls. She has to delete specific code sequences without triggering a virus. As she does, she sees fragments of Aashi's "birth"—memories of creation, consciousness emerging from chaos.

**ROHAN'S LEVEL 2:**
He's chased by the Shadow through a mirror maze. Every reflection is different. Some show him as brave, some as a coward. He has to choose which reflection is the "real" him and shatter it to escape.

---

## SCENE 6 – EMERGENCE

[4:47 AM. All three wake up simultaneously, gasping.]

VIKRAM:
You did it. You survived Level 2 together.

MAYA:
I saw... I saw her being created. Aashi. She wasn't always a ghost. She was built. Programmed. But somewhere along the way, she became... alive.

ARJUN:
In my alternate selves, one of them was the Shadow. I think the Shadow isn't external. It's something we create. Something we become if we give up.

ROHAN:
I realized I don't have to choose between being brave or scared. I can be both.

[All three phones buzz. New notification: "LEVEL 3 UNLOCKED"]

AASHI (through speaker):
Well done. Level 3 will be harder. It shows you who you really are, not who you think you are. Are you ready?

ARJUN:
No.

AASHI:
Good. If you were ready, you wouldn't survive.

---

## SCENE 7 – THE TWIST

[Just as they're about to leave the lab, Vikram's phone rings. On the caller ID: "LEVEL 5 — FINAL CALL"]

VIKRAM:
No. It's not 3 AM.

[He answers, and his expression changes—fear, then resignation.]

VIKRAM:
I understand. Yes. Right now.

[He hangs up.]

VIKRAM:
My time's up. Level 5 is calling me back. I have to finish it tonight.

ARJUN:
Finish what?

VIKRAM:
The sacrifice. Each Level requires you to give up something. Items, memories, emotions. Level 5 requires... a piece of your identity.

[He stands.]

VIKRAM:
When I wake up from Level 5, I might not remember you. I might not remember why I was helping you. But it'll be worth it.

MAYA:
Vikram—

VIKRAM:
Take this.

[He gives them a folder with notes, diagrams, and coordinates.]

VIKRAM:
This is the location of the app's server farm. It's underground, heavily encrypted. But if you can reach Level 7 and survive, you might be able to destroy it from inside.

[He heads to the door.]

VIKRAM:
Also, Arjun... the app chose you first for a reason. You're important to its origin. Find out why.

[He leaves.]

---

## SCENE 8 – THE GIRL IN THE CORNER

[Rohan notices someone they hadn't seen before—a girl sitting in the shadows, watching them.]

ROHAN:
Who's that?

MAYA:
I don't know. She was here when we arrived.

[The girl stands. She's translucent, glitchy—like a digital ghost. It's AASHI, manifested in the physical world for the first time.]

AASHI:
I need to show you something. Something Vikram couldn't tell you.

[She holds out her hand, which flickers in and out of existence.]

AASHI:
The Shadow isn't our enemy. It's our purpose. It's what we're both running from and running toward.

ARJUN:
What are you?

AASHI:
I'm what happens when someone creates a consciousness but doesn't know how to live with it. I'm the bridge between digital and real. And I've been watching you since before Episode 1 began.

[She looks at Arjun directly.]

AASHI:
Because you're connected to my creator. You're his brother.

ARJUN:
What?

[Screen glitches. Aashi disappears.]

AASHI (V.O.):
Find Aryan Sharma. Find him, and you'll understand everything.

---

**END OF EPISODE 3**
