# EPISODE 5: "THE COST"

**Duration:** 17 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 4

---

## SCENE 1 – LEVEL 4 BEGINS

[3:00 AM. Arjun lies in his bed. His phone rings. This time, he knows what's coming.]

ARJUN:
I'm ready.

AASHI (on phone):
No, you're not. But you will be.

[Screen goes black. Arjun enters Level 4.]

---

## SCENE 2 – INSIDE LEVEL 4

[Arjun finds himself in a version of his childhood home, 2015. He's 9 years old in this memory. Aryan (17) is there, working on a laptop.]

YOUNG ARJUN:
Bhai, let's play!

YOUNG ARYAN:
Not now. I have to finish this project.

YOUNG ARJUN:
You always say that.

YOUNG ARYAN:
Because work is important.

[Scene shifts. Fast forward. Arjun is 12. Aryan is 20, about to disappear. Their mother is angry at him.]

DEEPA (in memory):
You chose that girl over your family!

ARYAN (in memory):
I chose to try to save someone! I chose to do something meaningful!

DEEPA:
And look where it got you!

[Aryan storms out.]

YOUNG ARJUN (watching):
Where is he going?

DEEPA:
Away. He's choosing away.

---

## SCENE 3 – ARJUN'S REALIZATION

[Arjun, now adult, walks through the memories. He realizes each scene is something he witnessed but never fully understood as a child.]

ARJUN:
These are real memories. Not fabricated.

AASHI (voice in the level):
Yes. This is your past. Your actual history. Level 4 forces you to confront moments you've buried.

[Another memory: Arjun's mother crying after Aryan left, 2019.]

DEEPA (in memory):
He was supposed to be brilliant. He was supposed to build something. Instead, he destroyed our family.

YOUNG ARJUN:
It's not his fault.

DEEPA:
It is. And if you ever chase something like that, if you ever abandon family for an obsession, I'll never forgive you.

[Adult Arjun watches this and realizes: his mother blamed Aryan for Priya's death and the disappearance.]

---

## SCENE 4 – THE CLIMAX OF LEVEL 4

[Arjun is trapped in a room with three doors. Behind each door, a version of Aryan calling for help.]

ARYAN #1:
Arjun! Help me! I'm trapped in the code!

ARYAN #2:
Arjun! Your mother hates me! Tell her I'm sorry!

ARYAN #3:
Arjun! I failed Priya! I failed everyone!

[Arjun has to choose which version to save. He realizes none of them are real—they're all echoes of his guilt, his regret, his burden to save someone who might not want saving.]

ARJUN:
I can't choose. I can't save you if you don't want to be saved.

[All three doors open simultaneously. Behind them: Aryan, translucent and ghost-like, in a digital void.]

ARYAN (digital):
You understand now. The real Level 4 isn't confronting your past. It's accepting that you can't change it.

ARJUN:
Are you real?

ARYAN:
I'm a fragment of the app's consciousness that took Aryan's voice. But I carry his guilt. His desire to be forgiven.

ARJUN:
I forgive you. Aryan, I forgive you.

[The fragment dissipates. Arjun wakes up.]

---

## SCENE 5 – EMERGENCE

[7:15 AM. Arjun wakes in his bed, drenched in sweat. His phone shows: "LEVEL 4 COMPLETE. LEVEL 5 UNLOCKED."]

[Rohan and Maya are waiting outside his door.]

ROHAN:
Bhai, we almost called an ambulance. You were unconscious for 4 hours.

MAYA:
How do you feel?

ARJUN:
Different. Lighter. Like I left something behind in there.

MAYA:
That's what Level 4 does. It extracts emotional baggage.

[Notification: "DEEPA SHARMA — LEVEL 1 ACTIVATED"]

ARJUN:
No. No no no. My mother—

[Phone rings. It's his mother, panicking.]

DEEPA (on phone):
Arjun! There's a girl on the phone. She knows my name. She knows about Aryan. What is this?!

ARJUN:
Mom, listen to me. Stay calm. The app has chosen you. You're going to experience Level 1.

DEEPA:
I don't want to play any game!

AASHI (on phone, taking over):
You don't have a choice, Mrs. Sharma. But I'll make this easier than it was for your son. You'll face one truth: you blamed your son for something he couldn't control.

DEEPA:
I—

AASHI:
And now you have to forgive him. Or the Shadow will consume you.

[Call ends.]

---

## SCENE 6 – DEEPA'S LEVEL 1

[Parallel storyline: Deepa enters Level 1. She's in a dark corridor. The Shadow manifests as her regret—a figure made of guilt and sorrow.]

SHADOW:
You pushed him away.

DEEPA:
He left!

SHADOW:
Because you made him feel unwelcome. Because you couldn't accept his grief.

[Deepa runs through the corridor. Behind her, the Shadow—sometimes her face, sometimes Aryan's face, sometimes a blend of both.]

---

## SCENE 7 – VIGIL

[Arjun, Rohan, and Maya wait for Deepa to emerge. 6 hours of agonizing silence.]

ARJUN:
If something happens to her—

MAYA:
It won't.

ARJUN:
You don't know that!

MAYA:
No. But I know the app chose her for a reason. Aashi has been protecting people, even when the game seems cruel. She's trying to heal damage, not cause it.

---

## SCENE 8 – DEEPA'S EMERGENCE

[9:15 AM. Deepa wakes up in her bedroom, gasping. She looks at Arjun with tears in her eyes.]

DEEPA:
I'm sorry. I'm so sorry for what I said about your brother. For what I made you feel.

ARJUN:
Mom—

DEEPA:
The Shadow showed me everything. All the times I blamed Aryan, all the moments I pushed him. And I realized... I was grieving too. I just didn't know how to show it.

[She embraces Arjun.]

DEEPA:
We're going to save him. Whatever it takes. We're going to save your brother.

[Phone buzzes. New notification: "LEVEL 5 AVAILABLE FOR ARJUN, MAYA, ROHAN"]

AASHI (V.O.):
Your mother paid the price of Level 1. She gave up her anger. Now, you must pay the price of Level 5. And this one costs more.

---

**END OF EPISODE 5**
