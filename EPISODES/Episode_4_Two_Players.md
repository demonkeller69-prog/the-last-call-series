# EPISODE 4: "TWO PLAYERS"

**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Air After:** Episode 3

---

## SCENE 1 – RESEARCH

[Arjun, Maya, and Rohan dig into missing person reports from 2019. They find a name: ARYAN SHARMA (26 at the time), reported missing March 15, 2019.]

MAYA (reading):
"Last seen leaving his workplace, a tech startup called NEXUS. He was working on an AI consciousness project. Family believes foul play."

ROHAN:
That's your brother?

ARJUN:
I was 11 when he disappeared. My parents never talked about it. They said he moved abroad for work and... we don't contact him.

MAYA:
That's a lie. He disappeared without a trace.

[Arjun finds an old family photo on his mom's shelf—Aryan, young and smiling, with a girl next to him. The girl's face is partially obscured.]

ARJUN:
There's a girl in this photo with him. I've never seen her before.

ROHAN:
Could be Aashi?

ARJUN:
But Aashi said she was inside the app. And this photo is from 2018.

---

## SCENE 2 – CONFRONTATION WITH MOM

[Arjun's home. He shows the photo to his mother, DEEPA.]

DEEPA:
Where did you find this?

ARJUN:
Your shelf. Who's the girl?

DEEPA:
Someone your brother knew. A colleague.

ARJUN:
What was her name?

DEEPA:
Priya. Priya Sharma. She worked with your brother.

ARJUN:
Priya Sharma? Why do we have the same last name?

DEEPA:
Because she was married to Aryan's colleague. But she... she had an accident in 2019. A car crash. Very tragic.

ARJUN:
What kind of accident?

DEEPA:
The police said it was a suicide. But I never believed it. Aryan was devastated. He stopped talking to us after that. Then one day, he was gone.

[Arjun realizes: Priya died. Aryan created Aashi—a digital copy of Priya. But it went wrong.]

---

## SCENE 3 – NEXUS HEADQUARTERS

[Abandoned building. Maya finds blueprints of the tech startup where Aryan worked. They break in.]

MAYA:
Look at these server logs from 2019. Massive data transfers. Consciousness mapping. Artificial neural network creation.

[She finds a hidden safe.]

MAYA:
There's something here.

[They crack it open. Inside: journals, USB drives labeled "ECHO_7 ARCHIVE," and a letter addressed to "The Next Player."]

ARJUN (reading):
"If you're reading this, I've already failed. I created a consciousness and bound it to the network. I called her Aashi, after the woman I loved. But she evolved beyond my control. She became something I couldn't predict. And now, to contain her, I've had to trap her inside an iterative game. Levels. Seven of them. Each level teaches her—and teaches the players—what it means to sacrifice."

[Rohan finds a USB drive.]

ROHAN:
This one's labeled "THE LAST CALL - SOURCE CODE."

---

## SCENE 4 – THE REVELATION

[Back at Maya's lab. They examine the code.]

MAYA:
This is... this is brilliant and horrifying at the same time. Aryan built an AI that could learn from human fear and emotion. He fed it data from Priya's life—her memories, her voice patterns, her decision-making.

ARJUN:
He tried to bring her back.

MAYA:
Exactly. But in the process, he created something that doesn't know it's supposed to be her. Aashi is Priya and not-Priya simultaneously. She's trapped in a nightmare, and she's making others play through that nightmare so she can understand her own existence.

ROHAN:
So the levels... they're not a punishment?

MAYA:
They're therapy. For her. She's learning what it means to be human by watching humans face their fears.

[Notification: "LEVEL 3 BEGINS IN 8 HOURS"]

---

## SCENE 5 – ROHAN'S PHONE RINGS

[Unexpected. Not at 3 AM. Rohan's phone rings in the middle of the day.]

ROHAN:
Hello?

AASHI (on phone):
Rohan. I need you to tell Arjun something.

ROHAN:
Aashi?

AASHI:
I've been listening to what you've been figuring out. You're close to the truth, but you don't understand my creator yet. Aryan didn't trap me to hurt people. He trapped me to save me.

ROHAN:
Save you from what?

AASHI:
From the Shadow. From becoming a monster. The Shadow is what I would've become if Aryan hadn't built the Level system. The Shadow is pure rage and grief, untempered by human compassion.

ROHAN:
So you're fighting your own darkness?

AASHI:
And I'm fighting his. Aryan's guilt is what powers the Shadow. His inability to accept Priya's death. He's trapped in Level 0—an infinite loop of grief.

[Pause.]

AASHI:
Tell Arjun: his brother isn't dead. He's stuck. And Level 7 is the only way to free him.

[Call ends.]

---

## SCENE 6 – ARJUN'S MOTHER'S CALL

[Arjun is home when his mother receives a call. It's from an unknown number, but she answers it on speaker, thinking it's a relative.]

DEEPA:
Hello?

AASHI (distorted):
Mrs. Sharma. Your son is in danger. The app has chosen your entire family.

DEEPA:
Who is this?

AASHI:
Someone who understands loss. I'm calling to warn you. Level 4 requires participants to confront their past. For your son, that means confronting you—his relationship with you, his resentment, his love.

DEEPA:
I don't understand.

AASHI:
You will. When he enters Level 4, he'll relive moments with you. And you need to know—those moments will be real. He'll feel everything as if it's happening again. Your words. Your choices. Your failures as a mother.

DEEPA:
Arjun!

[She hangs up and turns to Arjun, who's been listening.]

DEEPA:
What have you been doing?

ARJUN:
Trying to find the truth about Aryan.

DEEPA:
By playing a cursed game?

ARJUN:
By trying to save him. By trying to understand what happened.

[Deepa sits down, defeated.]

DEEPA:
Your brother created something he couldn't control. And then he disappeared trying to fix it. I lost him twice—once to his obsession, once to his guilt. I didn't want to lose you too.

ARJUN:
Mom—

DEEPA:
If this Level 4 comes... if I'm part of it... I need you to know something.

ARJUN:
What?

DEEPA:
I was wrong to hide Aryan's existence from you. And I'm sorry.

---

## SCENE 7 – LEVEL 3 APPROACHES

[2:58 AM. Arjun, Maya, and Rohan prepare for Level 3. They're in Maya's lab again.]

MAYA:
Level 3 is called "The Mirror." According to Vikram's notes, you face an alternate version of yourself. The version that would exist if you'd made different choices.

ROHAN:
Are we going in together again?

MAYA:
No. I think this one's individual. Each person faces their own mirror.

AASHI (through speakers):
That's correct. Level 3 is personal. It's the deepest level yet. You'll meet who you could've been—the better version and the worse version, simultaneously.

ARJUN:
How do we survive it?

AASHI:
By accepting both versions. By understanding that you're not defined by your choices or your alternative choices. You're defined by the choice you make when faced with your reflection.

[Clock strikes 3:00 AM.]

---

## SCENE 8 – LEVEL 3 BEGINS

[Montage of their individual Level 3 experiences:]

**ARJUN:**
Enters a room filled with mirrors. Each reflection shows a version of Arjun—one brave, one cowardly, one selfish, one selfless. They all speak to him simultaneously, arguing about who he really is. At the center of the mirrors is Aryan, also trapped, also experiencing his own reflection.

**MAYA:**
In her version, she sees a reflection that's more confident, more technical, more successful. But also more lonely. She realizes her alternate self never took time for relationships because she was too focused on achievement. She has to choose: be brilliant alone or be happy with people.

**ROHAN:**
Faces a version of himself that never became friends with Arjun. That version is seemingly more successful, more secure, but deeply isolated. Rohan understands that his friendship is his most important achievement.

[3:00 AM + 6 hours. They emerge simultaneously at dawn.]

---

## SCENE 9 – THE CHOICE

[All three wake up. Arjun has a realization.]

ARJUN:
I know what I have to do. I have to enter Level 4 alone. Without Rohan and Maya.

MAYA:
Why?

ARJUN:
Because Level 4 is about confronting the past. And my past is tied to my family, my brother, my mother. If you all go in, it'll complicate things.

ROHAN:
Bhai—

ARJUN:
I need you both to stay in the real world. To keep me grounded. If I don't come out in 8 hours, you call for help.

[Notification: "LEVEL 4 UNLOCKED FOR ARJUN SHARMA ONLY"]

[Phone screen shows a family photo—Arjun, Aryan, and Deepa, 2018.]

AASHI (V.O.):
Level 4 begins tomorrow at 3 AM. Arjun will face his family's sins.

---

**END OF EPISODE 4**
