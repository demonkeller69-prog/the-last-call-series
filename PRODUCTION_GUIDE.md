## Camera & Cinematography

### SUGGESTED EQUIPMENT
- **Camera:** Sony FX30 / RED Komodo / Panasonic S1H
- **Lenses:** 24mm, 35mm, 50mm, 85mm for intimate scenes; 16mm for wide establishing
- **Lighting:** Aputure MC 4-Light kit, Neewer LED panels for tech scenes
- **Audio:** Rode Wireless GO II, Sennheiser MKE 600, Tascam DR-100mkIII
- **Stabilization:** DJI RS 3 gimbal, tripods, sliders for smooth tracking

### SHOT TYPES

**Real World Scenes:**
- Handheld for tension/fear (Levels)
- Locked-off for calm domestic scenes
- Over-shoulder for conversations
- POV shots from phone screens
- Slow push-ins for emphasis

**Level Sequences:**
- Distorted wide-angle lenses
- Heavy use of Dutch angles
- Glitch/digital effects in post
- Practical lighting (neon, fluorescent)
- Negative space emphasis

## Lighting Design

### EPISODE-SPECIFIC LIGHTING

**Episode 1 - Horror Focus**
- High contrast, deep shadows
- Practical phone lighting
- Dark ambient, sudden bright flashes
- Cool color temperature (3200K)

**Episodes 2-4 - Gradual Darkness**
- Balanced, naturalistic day lighting
- Increasingly moody night scenes
- Neon accents in tech spaces
- 4500K-5600K progression

**Episodes 5-7 - Digital Invasion**
- Heavy use of blue/cyan neon
- Unnatural lighting (glitchy, strobe-like)
- Green screens of monitors reflected on faces
- 6500K+ color temperature

**Episode 8 - Transcendence**
- Blinding white (hospital, server room)
- Gradual increase in luminosity
- Soft, diffused light in finale
- Warm (3200K) in emotional scenes

## Sound Design Strategy

### CORE AUDIO ELEMENTS

**Phone Calls**
- Aashi's voice: ethereal, sometimes distorted
- Background digital artifacts
- Glitching sound effects
- Call disconnection sounds (signature element)

**Ambient Sounds**
- Urban environment (traffic, distant voices)
- Tech sounds (keyboard clicks, server hum)
- Natural ambience (rain, wind)
- Silence (crucial for tension)

**Music**
- Minimal orchestral themes
- Electronic/ambient score
- Indian classical instruments (sitar, sarangi) mixed with synth
- Silence used as tool

**The Shadow**
- Whispered voice
- Reversed audio
- Heavy distortion
- Sound that's almost organic but slightly wrong

## Risk Management

### POTENTIAL ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

**Issue:** Bad weather during outdoor shoots  
**Solution:** Have indoor alternate scenes ready; build buffer days

**Issue:** Actor availability conflicts  
**Solution:** Plan shooting schedule well in advance; have stand-in options

**Issue:** VFX rendering delays  
**Solution:** Begin VFX work early; have fallback practical effects

**Issue:** Location access problems  
**Solution:** Scout alternative locations; secure permits early

**Issue:** Technical issues during filming  
**Solution:** Backup camera gear; redundant recording systems
