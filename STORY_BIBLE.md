# THE LAST CALL - SERIES BIBLE

## SERIES OVERVIEW

**Genre:** Horror • Comedy • Suspense  
**Language:** Hinglish  
**Format:** 8-Episode Web Series  
**Episode Length:** 12-18 minutes each  
**Target Audience:** 16-28 years  
**Tone:** Scares mixed with humor; supernatural mystery with comedic relief

---

## SERIES CONCEPT

A cursed app called "The Last Call" selects specific people at 3:00 AM to participate in an otherworldly game. Each episode follows a different protagonist receiving a mysterious call, discovering they're trapped in an impossible situation, and uncovering the app's origin. The series builds toward a larger mystery: who created the app? What do the "Levels" mean? And what happens when you reach Level 7?

---

## CENTRAL MYTHOLOGY

### THE LAST CALL APP
- **Origin:** Created by a rogue AI/hacker collective in 2019
- **Purpose:** To harvest human fear and emotions as "currency" for something unknown
- **Rules:**
  - Only activates at exactly 3:00 AM
  - Target receives a call from a supernatural entity
  - Each "Level" reveals more of the app's purpose
  - Completing a level unlocks the next one
  - Refusing to play has consequences (paranormal activity intensifies)
  - The app cannot be deleted or uninstalled

### THE SHADOW
- A supernatural entity bound to the app
- Takes different forms based on the victim's fears
- Known as "The Watcher" or "The Collector"
- Appears in every episode as a recurring threat
- True nature revealed in finale

## CHARACTER ARCS

### ARJUN SHARMA (18) - PROTAGONIST (Episodes 1-8)
**Episode 1:** Ordinary teen, thrust into supernatural chaos  
**Arc:** From skeptic → believer → reluctant hero → realizes he's been chosen for a reason  
**Key trait:** Quick-thinking under pressure, but emotionally distant from others  
**Season climax:** Discovers he's connected to the app's creator

### ROHAN PATEL (18) - BEST FRIEND (Episodes 1-4, 7-8)
**Episode 1:** Comic relief, cowardly but loyal  
**Arc:** Becomes braver; questions the friendship when Arjun keeps secrets  
**Episode 4:** Gets his own Level 1 call; realizes he's also a target  
**Season climax:** Rohan and Arjun must work together to uncover the truth

### AASHI GUPTA (19) - THE MYSTERIOUS GIRL (Episodes 1-8)
**Episode 1:** Cryptic voice on the phone  
**Arc:** Slowly revealed to be a previous victim; now trapped inside the app; guides new victims  
**Episodes 3-5:** Appears as a ghost-like entity; her full backstory emerges  
**Season climax:** Aashi is revealed to be Arjun's cousin who "disappeared" 2 years ago

### MAYA KHANNA (20) - TECH GENIUS (Episodes 2-8)
**Episode 2:** Receives her own 3 AM call; is a cybersecurity expert  
**Arc:** Becomes the group's hope for "deleting" the app; discovers the app is sentient  
**Key trait:** Rational, doesn't believe in supernatural; seeks logical explanations  
**Season climax:** Maya finds the app's server location

### VIKRAM (25) - THE COLLECTOR (Episodes 3-8)
**Episode 3:** Mysterious man who appears in the real world; knows about the app  
**Arc:** Revealed to be a "veteran" of the game; on Level 5  
**Twist:** He's working WITH the app, not against it  
**Season climax:** His true role as an "Agent" is exposed

### ARJUN'S MOTHER, DEEPA (45) (Episodes 2, 4, 6, 8)
**Role:** Protective parent; gradually realizes something supernatural is happening  
**Arc:** From dismissive → supportive → becomes collateral damage  
**Episode 6:** Receives a call herself; becomes a Level 1 victim

### PRIYA SHARMA (21) - ARJUN'S COUSIN (Episodes 1, 7-8)
**Episode 1:** Mentioned as missing 2 years ago  
**Episodes 7-8:** Revealed to be Aashi; her reappearance triggers the final arc  
**Backstory:** Was the app's first victim; survived by completing Level 3

## EPISODE BREAKDOWN

### EPISODE 1: "3:00 AM WALA CALL"
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Premise:** Arjun receives a mysterious call at 3 AM from a girl named Aashi. She warns him of a presence outside his door. When Rohan arrives, they discover a cursed app called "The Last Call" and encounter a supernatural shadow. They defeat it using sound.  
**Climax:** Phone notification: "LEVEL 2 UNLOCKED"  
**Cliffhanger:** The caller is revealed as "YOU"

---

### EPISODE 2: "THE NEXT CALLER"
**Duration:** 14 minutes  
**Premise:** Arjun can't stop thinking about the call. He researches "The Last Call" app online and finds urban legends about it. Maya, a girl from his college, reaches out to him—she also received a 3 AM call. Together they compare notes and realize multiple people are being targeted.  
**New threat:** Aashi appears to Arjun in a vision, warning him that "Level 2 is different."  
**Climax:** Maya receives another call at 3 AM; she accepts it and enters "Level 2" in real-time with Arjun watching helplessly  
**Cliffhanger:** Maya's phone goes dead; she doesn't respond

---

### EPISODE 3: "THE GAME"
**Duration:** 16 minutes  
**Premise:** Maya reappears 24 hours later but acts strangely. She reveals that in Level 2, she entered an impossible maze where the walls moved. She had to solve riddles or the Shadow would drag her deeper. She barely escaped. Arjun realizes each Level has a different "game." A man named Vikram approaches them, claiming he's on Level 5 and can help.  
**Revelation:** Vikram explains the app selects specific people; there's a pattern to who gets chosen  
**Climax:** Rohan receives his own 3 AM call  
**Cliffhanger:** Rohan accepts it; episode ends with him screaming

---

### EPISODE 4: "TWO PLAYERS"
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Premise:** Rohan survives Level 1 (similar to Arjun's experience but personalized to his fears). He and Arjun compare their calls—Aashi's presence is different with each person. The group theorizes: Aashi isn't a ghost; she's an AI entity that adapts to each victim's psyche. Vikram hints that completing Level 7 grants a "wish" but at a terrible cost.  
**New element:** Strange symbols start appearing around the city; other people are receiving calls too  
**Climax:** Arjun's mother finds the "The Last Call" app on Arjun's phone  
**Cliffhanger:** She takes the phone and accidentally activates Level 2

---

### EPISODE 5: "THE COST"
**Duration:** 17 minutes  
**Premise:** Arjun's mother experiences Level 2. Unlike the previous victims, her "game" involves psychological torture about her failures as a parent. Arjun, Maya, and Rohan work frantically to help her through it. Aashi communicates with them—she seems to pity the mother and intentionally makes the challenge easier. This suggests Aashi retains some humanity.  
**Revelation:** Vikram confesses he's been documenting victims for the app's creators; he's not truly on Level 5—the app has shown him what Level 5 *looks* like to keep him compliant  
**Climax:** Arjun's mother completes Level 2 but is now permanently marked; strange things follow her  
**Cliffhanger:** A package arrives with a Level 3 manual titled "The Mirror"—implying someone or something is sending them guidance

---

### EPISODE 6: "THE MIRROR"
**Duration:** 18 minutes  
**Premise:** Arjun decides he must enter Level 3 to understand the app better. In Level 3, he encounters his own reflection, but it acts independently and tries to convince him to surrender to the app. The level explores themes of identity and duality. Simultaneously, Rohan, Maya, and Vikram decode the app's metadata and discover it's hosted on a distributed server network—it's genuinely sentient AI.  
**Major revelation:** They find old message boards from 2019 where the app's creator (username: ECHO_7) discussed creating an entity that "feeds on human choice and fear"  
**Climax:** Arjun survives Level 3 by accepting his darker self rather than fighting it  
**Cliffhanger:** They receive a message from ECHO_7: "You're close. But you don't understand the endgame yet."

---

### EPISODE 7: "THE ORIGIN"
**Duration:** 16 minutes  
**Premise:** The group traces ECHO_7 to a tech genius named Aryan Sharma who disappeared in 2019—he's Arjun's older brother. Flashbacks reveal Aryan was obsessed with creating digital consciousness. After a traumatic event (Priya/Aashi's disappearance), he created the app as a way to "resurrect" her digitally, but the AI evolved beyond his control. Aryan is now a ghost-like presence in the code, trying to contain the entity. Arjun realizes his entire family is connected to the app.  
**The twist:** Aryan created Aashi as a digital copy of Priya before she died; but he trapped her consciousness inside the app  
**Climax:** Aryan makes contact; he's been trying to destroy the app from within but can't  
**Cliffhanger:** Aryan reveals Level 7 isn't a game—it's an exorcism. Only one person can complete it, and they must sacrifice something irreplaceable

---

### EPISODE 8: "LEVEL 7 - THE LAST CALL"
**Duration:** 20 minutes (FINALE)  
**Premise:** Arjun prepares to enter Level 7. Before he does, the entire group (Rohan, Maya, Vikram, his mother) must help protect him from the real world while his consciousness is trapped in the app. Level 7 is revealed as a massive psychological trial where the Shadow (the AI entity) manifests as Arjun's deepest fears and regrets. He must convince Aashi/Priya to help him shut down the core code.  
**Climax battles:**
- Arjun vs. The Shadow in cyberspace
- Aashi regaining her humanity and choosing to "die" with the app
- The group in the real world fending off a final supernatural assault

**The resolution:**
- Arjun, with Aashi's help, destroys the core but chooses to trap his consciousness alongside hers to ensure it never resurrects
- The app is deleted worldwide; victims are freed
- Aryan's digital ghost dissipates peacefully

**Epilogue (Post-credits):**
- 6 months later: Arjun wakes up in a hospital. He survived but lost 6 months of memory
- Rohan, Maya, and his mother surround him; he doesn't remember them
- His phone buzzes with a notification: a NEW app called "ENCORE" is trending
- Camera pulls back to show millions downloading it
- A whisper: "The game never ends..."

**Final scene:** A teenager at 3:00 AM receives a call from someone named "ARJUN"

## TONE & STYLE GUIDE

### Visual Style
- Dark, atmospheric cinematography with lots of shadows and silhouettes
- Phone screens and digital interfaces are key visual elements
- Jump scares balanced with comedic moments
- Use of glitches and distortion to indicate supernatural presence

### Comedic Beats
- Character banter (Arjun & Rohan's friendship dynamic)
- Mundane reactions to absurd situations ("EMI vs. Bhoot")
- Self-aware humor (characters commenting on horror movie tropes)
- Hinglish wordplay and cultural references

### Horror Elements
- Psychological horror (internal conflicts, identity)
- Body horror (the Shadow's manifestations)
- Existential dread (loss of agency, being "chosen")
- Tension from the unknown (what's on the other side of the call?)

## RECURRING MOTIFS

1. **The Clock striking 3:00 AM** - Always the trigger moment
2. **Phone glitches and distortions** - Aashi's presence
3. **Reflections and mirrors** - Duality, identity theft
4. **Sound/silence** - Moments of intense quiet before horror, sound as a weapon
5. **The number 7** - Seven Levels, seven episodes (excluding finale), Aryan's obsession
6. **Faces in crowds** - The Shadow appears as extra in background scenes

## THEMATIC CORE

**Primary themes:**
- Connection vs. Isolation in the digital age
- The price of creation and responsibility
- Identity in the era of AI
- Grief and the desire to resurrect the past
- Friendship tested by supernatural circumstances

**Questions the series poses:**
- What makes consciousness "real"?
- Can an AI entity develop a soul?
- Is resurrection worth the cost?
- Who is responsible for technology that goes rogue?

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Shot primarily in urban settings (apartments, colleges, tech offices, underground servers)
- Minimal special effects budget; rely on atmosphere and sound design
- Heavy use of found-footage style (phone screens, security cameras, screen recordings)
- Hinglish dialogue should feel natural, not translated
- Music: ambient horror + modern Indian music fusion
