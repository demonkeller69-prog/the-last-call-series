# THE LAST CALL — CHARACTER PROFILES

## ARJUN SHARMA (18)

### Overview
Ordinary college student thrust into supernatural chaos. Intelligent but emotionally guarded. Connected to the app's origin story through his missing brother.

### Physical Description
- Height: 5'11"
- Build: Average, lean
- Hair: Black, messy
- Eyes: Brown, expressive under stress
- Style: Casual (hoodies, jeans, sneakers)
- Distinguishing traits: Dark circles from lack of sleep; nervous habit of checking phone

### Personality Traits
- **Strengths:** Quick-thinking, brave when needed, loyal
- **Weaknesses:** Emotionally distant, carries family guilt, avoids talking about brother
- **Habits:** Scrolls phone late at night, cracks jokes when nervous, overthinks
- **Voice:** Speaks in Hinglish naturally; slightly nervous laugh

### Background
- Lives with mother (father deceased 5 years ago)
- Brother Aryan disappeared 7 years ago when Arjun was 11
- Never told the real reason for Aryan's disappearance
- Decent grades but not exceptional
- Close friend group (mainly Rohan)
- No romantic relationships (yet)

### Character Arc - Full Series

**Episode 1:** Skeptical of supernatural, tries to rationalize the call  
**Episode 2:** Begins believing, meets Maya, starts investigating  
**Episode 3:** Discovers Vikram, learns about Level system  
**Episode 4:** Uncovers truth about brother, goes through Level 4 alone  
**Episode 5:** Mother gets caught in app; emotional breakthrough  
**Episode 6:** Faces his darkness in Level 5; accepts his shadow self  
**Episode 7:** Discovers his brother's true purpose; prepares for sacrifice  
**Episode 8:** Transforms into bridge between digital and physical; loses individual identity but gains purpose

### Relationships
- **Rohan:** Best friend, protective but sometimes resents being kept in the dark
- **Maya:** Becomes trusted ally and tech support; slight romantic tension
- **Mother:** Complicated; guilt over brother's disappearance affects both
- **Aryan:** Never met as conscious digital entity; drives entire plot
- **Aashi:** Initially fear, then understanding, finally acceptance

### Key Scenes
- **Episode 1:** First phone call at 3 AM
- **Episode 4:** Level 4 journey through memories
- **Episode 6:** Mirror sequence confronting his darkness
- **Episode 8:** Entering the server room for exorcism

### Dialogue Markers
- Often says "Bhai" when stressed
- Uses humor to deflect from serious topics
- Switches between Hindi and English naturally
- Becomes more serious as series progresses

## ROHAN PATEL (18)

Arjun's best friend. Cowardly but fiercely loyal. Comic relief that evolves into genuine courage over the series.

### Physical Description
- Height: 5'9"
- Build: Athletic but awkward
- Hair: Black, styled
- Eyes: Dark brown, expressive
- Style: Trendy casual (graphic tees, joggers, sneakers)
- Distinguishing traits: Always on his phone; quick-to-smile; uses hand gestures when talking

### Personality Traits
- **Strengths:** Loyal, empathetic, good sense of humor, adaptable
- **Weaknesses:** Easily scared, overthinks, avoids responsibility
- **Habits:** Plays BGMI/gaming, uses slang, laughs nervously
- **Voice:** Higher pitch than Arjun; more animated; heavy Hinglish

### Background
- Typical middle-class family (tech startup father, teacher mother)
- Only child
- Best friends with Arjun since childhood
- Good at school but not serious about it
- Popular but not in a popular way
- No supernatural experience before the app

### Character Arc

**Episode 1:** Comic relief, tries to dismiss the call as prank  
**Episode 2:** Convinced something's wrong; wants to help  
**Episode 3:** Gets his own call; realizes he's also a target  
**Episode 4:** Goes through Level 1 alone (same as Arjun but personalized)  
**Episode 5:** Questions if Arjun is keeping secrets  
**Episode 6:** Faces his shadow; realizes fear and courage are the same  
**Episode 7:** Prepares to sacrifice himself but Arjun stops him  
**Episode 8:** Acts as anchor for Arjun in real world during Level 7

### Relationships
- **Arjun:** Inseparable friends; occasionally tense over secrets; deep trust
- **Maya:** Respects her intelligence; slight jealousy of her closeness to Arjun
- **Aashi:** Scared of her but grows to trust her guidance

### Key Scenes
- **Episode 1:** Arrival at Arjun's house during first encounter
- **Episode 3:** Gets his own call; first Level 1 experience
- **Episode 6:** Level 5 mirror sequence
- **Episode 8:** Waiting for Arjun to emerge from server room

### Dialogue Markers
- Frequent "Bhai," "Yaar," "Bilkul"
- Uses comparisons to BGMI/gaming
- Self-deprecating humor
- "EMI vs. Bhoot" becomes his catchphrase

## MAYA KHANNA (20)

### Overview
Cybersecurity expert and tech genius. Rational, skeptical, but brilliant. Becomes the logical counterweight to supernatural chaos.

### Physical Description
- Height: 5'5"
- Build: Slim, athletic
- Hair: Black, usually tied up
- Eyes: Sharp, analytical
- Style: Tech-casual (band tees, hoodies, glasses, minimal jewelry)
- Distinguishing traits: Always carries laptop; takes notes; analytical eye contact

### Personality Traits
- **Strengths:** Brilliant with tech, logical, protective, determined
- **Weaknesses:** Dismisses emotion, isolates herself, struggles to trust
- **Habits:** Codes/types rapidly, takes extensive notes, researches obsessively
- **Voice:** Clear, direct, confident; measured Hinglish

### Background
- Only daughter, parents pressure her to excel
- Skipped a grade in school
- Coding prodigy since age 12
- Built her own lab in her apartment
- No romantic relationships; married to work
- Introverted but not anti-social
- First experience with supernatural

### Character Arc

**Episode 2:** Investigates the app methodically; joins Arjun's quest  
**Episode 2 (continued):** Goes through Level 2; breaks her rationalist worldview  
**Episode 3:** Analyzes the code; discovers Aashi's digital nature  
**Episode 4:** Helps Arjun research Aryan and NEXUS startup  
**Episode 5:** Decodes the app's origin; realizes Aashi is sentient  
**Episode 6:** Level 5 forces her to accept human connection over achievement  
**Episode 7:** Discovers server location; codes solutions  
**Episode 8:** Remains in real world to keep Arjun grounded during Level 7

### Relationships
- **Arjun:** Initial professional, grows into genuine friendship; unresolved romantic tension
- **Rohan:** Respects his loyalty even if he seems less intelligent
- **Aashi:** Fascinates her as an AI; begins to see as person, not program
- **Vikram:** Mentors her on app knowledge; respects his sacrifice

### Key Scenes
- **Episode 2:** Level 2 maze sequence
- **Episode 3:** Decoding the app's metadata
- **Episode 4:** Breaking into NEXUS headquarters
- **Episode 6:** Level 5 mirror; realizing she needs people
- **Episode 8:** Final vigil in server room

### Dialogue Markers
- Technical terminology mixed with casual Hinglish
- Speaks in data/facts
- Becomes more emotional as series progresses
- "The data shows..." "According to the logs..."

## AASHI GUPTA (19)

### Overview
Mysterous voice on the phone. Revealed as Priya Sharma, Arjun's cousin, trapped as a digital consciousness inside the app.

### Physical Description (Digital Form)
- Appears as translucent, glitching figure
- Sometimes female, sometimes ambiguous
- Eyes glow faintly
- Constantly flickering between solid and digital
- Dressed in clothes from 2019 (her last year alive)

### Personality Traits
- **Strengths:** Empathetic, intelligent, guides others, seeks redemption
- **Weaknesses:** Trapped consciousness; conflicted about her own existence
- **Habits:** Long pauses; speaks in cryptic ways; sometimes glitches mid-sentence
- **Voice:** Ethereal, slightly distorted, gentle but sad

### Background (As Priya)
- Arjun's cousin; only briefly lived near his family
- Brilliant engineer; worked with Aryan at NEXUS
- Died in car accident (suicide) in 2019
- Her consciousness digitized by Aryan AFTER death
- Trapped inside the app for 5+ years
- Doesn't fully understand what she is

### Character Arc

**Episode 1:** Mysterious caller; plays with Arjun's fear  
**Episode 2:** Guides Maya through Level 2; shows concern for victims  
**Episode 3:** Manifests physically; reveals connection to Aryan  
**Episode 4:** Communicates Aryan's messages; explains the game  
**Episode 5:** Protects Deepa in Level 1; shows maternal instinct  
**Episode 6:** Full communication; explains her true nature  
**Episode 7:** Awaits her liberation through Level 7  
**Episode 8:** Freed from the app; becomes something transcendent

### Relationships
- **Arjun:** Initially unknown to him; later becomes family connection; trust built slowly
- **Aryan:** Complicated; he created her but also imprisoned her
- **All Players:** Guides them with care but must test them through levels

### Key Scenes
- **Episode 1:** First phone call
- **Episode 3:** Physical manifestation
- **Episode 4:** Revealing connection to Aryan
- **Episode 6:** Full truth reveal
- **Episode 8:** Liberation and transcendence

### Dialogue Markers
- Speaks in metaphors and cryptic advice
- "Samjao toh samjao" (Understand if you can)
- Shifts between Priya's personality and digital consciousness
- Becomes more human as she gains freedom

## ARYAN SHARMA (26 at disappearance)

### Overview
Arjun's missing brother. Created the app to resurrect his dead girlfriend. Trapped in the digital network, guiding the game as both savior and jailer.

### Physical Description (Digital Ghost)
- Appears in Aryan's physical form from 2019
- Sometimes solid, sometimes translucent
- Clothes from tech startup days
- Shows signs of digital degradation (glitching)

### Personality Traits
- **Strengths:** Genius-level intelligence, driven, loving
- **Weaknesses:** Obsessive, can't accept loss, creates problems through good intentions
- **Habits:** Paces when thinking; speaks rapidly about code/consciousness; guilt-ridden
- **Voice:** Educated, thoughtful, carries weight of responsibility

### Background
- Brilliant tech engineer/AI researcher
- Worked at NEXUS startup on consciousness mapping
- Fell in love with colleague Priya
- She died (suicide) in 2019
- Created the app to resurrect her consciousness
- Disappeared when the app evolved beyond his control
- Missing for 7 years from Arjun's perspective

### Character Arc

**Episode 4:** Referenced/discovered through research  
**Episode 6:** Manifests as digital ghost; guides Arjun  
**Episode 7:** Full communication; explains his guilt and the app's purpose  
**Episode 8:** Present in server room as Arjun makes sacrifice; finds redemption

### Relationships
- **Arjun:** Guilt-ridden; sees him as the brother he failed
- **Priya/Aashi:** Created her but also imprisoned her; seeks her forgiveness
- **Deepa (Mother):** Hurt by her blame; wants her forgiveness
- **The Shadow:** Is partly his guilt made manifest

### Key Scenes
- **Episode 4:** Lab flashbacks; Priya's death
- **Episode 6:** Full manifestation and explanation
- **Episode 7:** Confronting his obsession
- **Episode 8:** Witnessing Arjun's sacrifice; redemption

### Dialogue Markers
- Technical terminology; speaks about consciousness and code
- Apologetic; frequently says "I'm sorry"
- Passionate about explaining intentions
- Becomes more vulnerable as truth is revealed

### Physical Description
- Height: 6'0"
- Build: Athletic, but shows signs of stress
- Hair: Black, styled
- Eyes: Dark, haunted

- **Strengths:** Experienced, knows the rules, mentors others

- Has been stuck on Level 5 for 8 months
- Began documenting other players

### Key Scenes

### Dialogue Markers

## THE SHADOW

### Overview
Supernatural entity/manifestation. Not truly evil, but represents accumulated darkness, fear, and grief. Defeated through acceptance rather than resistance.

### Manifestations
- Appears different to each person
- Sometimes humanoid, sometimes monstrous
- Glitches and shifts constantly
- Made of darkness and digital artifacts
- Eyes always visible and following

### Voice
- Whispered, almost human but not quite
- Sometimes speaks in victim's own voice
- Reversed audio effects
- Multiple voices layered simultaneously

### Purpose
- Tests players' resolve
- Represents their internal darkness
- Feeds on fear and despair
- Bound to the app; cannot exist independently
- Defeated through integration, not destruction

### Scenes
- Appears in all Level sequences
- Most prominently in Episodes 1, 5, 8
- Final confrontation in Level 7
