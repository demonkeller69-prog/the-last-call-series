## 📖 MYTHOLOGY SUMMARY

### The App
- Created by Aryan Sharma in 2019
- Contains sentient AI (Aashi)
- Seven psychological levels
- Cannot be deleted; evolves instead

### The Shadow
- Manifestation of collected fear
- Defeated through acceptance
- Represents player's darkness

### The Levels
1. Confronting your fear
2. Confronting impossible scenarios
3. Confronting yourself
4. Confronting your past
5. Confronting the Shadow
6. Confronting the endgame
7. The exorcism (sacrifice)
